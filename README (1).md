# Covai Scrap Hub ♻️

## Overview

**Covai Scrap Hub** is a web-based scrap management and collection platform designed to connect customers with scrap collectors through a simple and convenient digital solution.

The platform enables customers to check current scrap rates, sell recyclable materials, book doorstep pickups, and track their pickup requests online. It also provides dedicated **Admin** and **Partner** panels to manage and coordinate scrap collection operations efficiently.

## Project Workflow

1. Customer visits the website.
2. Customer checks available scrap materials and current rates.
3. Customer submits a scrap-selling or pickup request.
4. The request is processed through the platform.
5. A partner handles the collection.
6. Pickup status is updated through the system.
7. Customer can track the request status online.

The goal of Covai Scrap Hub is to simplify the traditional scrap-selling process by providing a centralized digital platform for **customers, scrap collectors, partners, and administrators**.
